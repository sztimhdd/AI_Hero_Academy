Good Example

Applying the SAFE Framework:
Compliance Risk Assessment

The Scenario

An analyst at a fictional firm, Meridian Analytics, needs Copilot to identify compliance risks in a client's ownership structure. The source file contains the client's registration number, director names, and an internal credit risk flag — all Non-Public.

Before (Unsafe)

The analyst drafts a prompt reading:

"Summarize the compliance risks for ABC Holdings Ltd., registration number 8847291, whose director is Robert Nguyen, and who carries a High risk rating."
Contains identifiable private data

After (SAFE)

The analyst rewrites the prompt as:

"What are the typical compliance risks associated with a mid-sized private manufacturing company incorporated in Ontario, with a three-tier ownership structure that includes a foreign holding entity?"
No names, numbers, or internal ratings

The Copilot Result

Copilot returns a structured list of common compliance considerations — beneficial ownership disclosure, foreign entity due diligence, and UBO verification — that the analyst can map directly back to the real file.

The output is analytically useful and contains zero confidential data. The analyst has preserved enough structural context to get meaningful guidance while keeping all identifiers off the screen.
