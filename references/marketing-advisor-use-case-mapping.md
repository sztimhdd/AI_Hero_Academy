# AI Skills Training Program Design for Marketing Roles at EDC

This document outlines the selected AI use cases and course anchors for the AI skills training program tailored to the following roles at Export Development Canada (EDC):

*   Marketing Business Partner
*   Marketing Manager (including Digital, Brand, Demand Generation, and Content Marketing specializations)
*   Marketing Operations Lead/Manager
*   Marketing Automation Specialist/Manager
*   Senior Advisor, Marketing Planning & Operations
*   Communications Advisor (in Corporate Communications)

The program is structured around six fixed skill domains, with one course per domain and a capstone course that integrates all six.

## Task 3 — Proposed Course Anchors

### Course 1 – Responsible AI

*   course\_id: mk\_c1\_responsible\_ai
*   Use case: The main objective of this case is to obtain approval to input internal (i.e., non-public) client data into MS Copilot on the Web to increase work efficiency on the Impact team (FinDev Canada).
*   real\_use\_case: The main objective of this case is to obtain approval to input internal (i.e., non-public) client data into MS Copilot on the Web to increase work efficiency on the Impact team (FinDev Canada).
*   Rationale: This is the closest library match to the marketer’s real risk of putting prospect lists, CRM notes, or campaign data into AI without applying the public/non-public test first.
*   Suggested title: Protect Non-Public Marketing Data in AI

### Course 2 – Strategic Prompting

*   course\_id: mk\_c2\_strategic\_prompting
*   Use case: Content Creation & Lifecycle
*   real\_use\_case: Content Creation & Lifecycle
*   Rationale: This connects directly to the role’s daily task of generating first drafts for campaign copy, web content, and sales enablement assets from well-structured prompts.
*   Suggested title: Prompt Better Marketing Drafts Faster

### Course 3 – Critical Evaluation

*   course\_id: mk\_c3\_critical\_eval
*   Use case: Customer Interaction Recap
*   real\_use\_case: Customer Interaction Recap
*   Rationale: This matches the role’s need to review AI-generated recaps of calls, emails, and CRM notes before using them for follow-up, planning, or reporting.
*   Suggested title: Review AI Recaps Before You Act

### Course 4 – Relationship Intelligence

*   course\_id: mk\_c4\_relationship\_intel
*   Use case: Prospect Intelligence
*   real\_use\_case: Prospect Intelligence
*   Rationale: This maps directly to marketing’s prospecting and account-based marketing work, where richer prospect context improves relevance and timing of outreach.
*   Suggested title: Build Stronger Outreach With Prospect Insight

### Course 5 – Data-Driven Decision Making

*   course\_id: mk\_c5\_data\_decision
*   Use case: Streamline production of Quarterly analysis and reporting
*   real\_use\_case: Streamline production of Quarterly analysis and reporting
*   Rationale: This fits the role’s recurring work of pulling campaign metrics, compiling trends, and turning performance data into decision-ready summaries for leadership.
*   Suggested title: Turn Campaign Data Into Better Decisions

### Course 6 – Augmented Communication

*   course\_id: mk\_c6\_augmented\_comm
*   Use case: Corporate Communications (translation, messaging)
*   real\_use\_case: Corporate Communications (translation, messaging)
*   Rationale: This reflects the role’s responsibility for bilingual messaging, internal/external communications, and adapting content for different audiences and channels.
*   Suggested title: Draft Clear Bilingual Marketing Communications

### Course 7 – Capstone

*   course\_id: mk\_c7\_capstone
*   Use case(s): Exporter Journey Optimization Agent; Automating Prospect Profiling; Customer Interaction Recap; Streamline production of Quarterly analysis and reporting; Corporate Communications (translation, messaging)
*   real\_use\_case: Exporter Journey Optimization Agent; Automating Prospect Profiling; Customer Interaction Recap; Streamline production of Quarterly analysis and reporting; Corporate Communications (translation, messaging)
*   Rationale: Together these use cases form a realistic end-to-end marketing workflow that integrates safe data handling, prompting, recap review, prospect personalization, performance analysis, and audience-ready communications.
*   Suggested title: Run an AI-Assisted Marketing Workflow End-to-End
