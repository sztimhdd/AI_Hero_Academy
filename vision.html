Core IP — AI Coach

Not a chatbot. A coach.

P

Purpose

Declare the learning objective before generating a question. No question without a purpose.

A

Assess

Read both the intellectual signal (what they understood) and the emotional signal (how they feel).

C

Choose

Select the right coaching move — Challenge · Clarify · Celebrate · Support — based on the assessment.

E

Exit

Close the task the moment the objective is met. Never ask an unnecessary question. Never linger.

3-Question Budget — Hard Ceiling
1
Open probe — surface current thinking (always)
2
Adaptive — challenge if shallow / affirm if good
3
Synthesis — consolidate + bridge to build artifact
4
Blocked. Budget exhausted → give insight directly.
Emotional Detection — 5 Patterns
Short / dismissiveReframe without pressure
FrustratedStop. Give insight directly.
OverconfidentGentle challenge: push deeper
Genuine insightCelebrate + close. No more Qs.
Confused / lostSimplify + ground in their work context
